Organization Structure Guidelines

Company → Departments → Teams → Team Lead (TL) + Members

  • Create Departments (e.g., Engineering, Sales, HR, Quality Assurance)
  • Create Teams under designated departments
  • Assign an active Team Lead (TL) to each team
  • Register and assign Employees to teams
  • Create Projects, map them to teams, and designate project owners
